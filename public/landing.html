The CCI

Meet the instrument that was built for you.

The Capacity Clinical Instrument (CCI) is a psychometrically-grounded daily check-in designed specifically for neurodivergent nervous systems. It doesn't measure mood. It doesn't measure productivity.

It measures capacity — the energy available for thinking, feeling, and functioning. The thing you've always sensed but never had language for. One check-in per day. A 90-day longitudinal record that grows more clinically meaningful with every entry.
